About Cartly

We started with one simple idea: online shopping in Nigeria should be easy, honest and affordable.

Our story

From a small shop in Lagos to thousands of homes

Cartly began in 2020 as a small shop in Lekki. Our customers kept asking if they could order from home, so we built a simple online store. Today we deliver to every state in Nigeria.

We work directly with trusted suppliers, so every product you buy is original and fairly priced.

  • 100% original products
  • Delivery to all 36 states
  • Friendly, real human support

What drives us

Three simple ideas guide everything we do.

Our mission

To make quality products easy to find, easy to buy and fast to receive for every Nigerian.

Our vision

To become the most trusted online store in Africa, known for honesty and great service.

Our values

Honesty, fair prices and putting our customers first in every decision we make.
